Action Quest

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Action Quest is a Discord bot built for Action Model's community campaigns. This page explains what data the bot collects, why, and how it's handled.

Data we collect

  • Discord User ID — to associate your linked X account and role eligibility with your Discord account.
  • X (Twitter) username — the username you provide via /link-x, used only to verify that posts you submit were authored by you.
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID — to store per-server campaign configuration: channel, reward role, excluded role, and active status.
  • Submitted post links and timestamps — the X post URL you paste in the campaign channel, logged for record-keeping and dispute resolution.

What we don't collect

  • We don't access your X account, DMs, email, password, or anything beyond the public post URL you voluntarily submit.
  • We don't read message content outside the designated campaign channel.
  • We don't use AI, and we don't send any data to third-party AI services.
  • We don't make calls to the X/Twitter API.

How data is used

Data is used only to operate the campaign: verifying that a submitted post belongs to the linked account, enforcing exclusion rules, and assigning Discord roles. Submission logs may be reviewed by server admins for moderation purposes.

Data storage

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway. It's retained for as long as the bot remains active in a server, or until a user or admin requests deletion.

Data sharing

We don't sell or share collected data with third parties. Data is only accessible to the bot's operators for administration and debugging.

Your rights

You can request removal of your linked X account and submission history at any time by contacting a server admin, or by reaching out directly using the contact below.
